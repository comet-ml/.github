# Support

Thanks for using Comet's open-source tools. Here's the fastest route to an answer, depending on what you need.

## Documentation first

- [Comet documentation](https://www.comet.com/docs) — platform, SDKs, and integrations
- [Opik documentation](https://www.comet.com/docs/opik) — LLM tracing and evaluation
- [comet-examples](https://github.com/comet-ml/comet-examples) — runnable notebooks and end-to-end pipelines

## Questions and discussion

Join the [Comet Slack community](https://www.comet.com/slack). It's the best place for "how do I…" questions, setup help, and feedback on where the tools should go next.

## Bugs and feature requests

Open an issue **on the repository the problem is in** — for example [opik](https://github.com/comet-ml/opik/issues) or [opik-mcp](https://github.com/comet-ml/opik-mcp/issues). Search existing issues first; if you find a match, add your details there rather than opening a duplicate.

A good report includes your version, your environment, what you expected, what happened, and the smallest snippet that reproduces it.

## Security issues

Do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) to report privately.

## Commercial support

Using Comet's hosted platform on a paid plan? Contact your account team or reach out through [comet.com](https://www.comet.com) for support backed by an SLA.
